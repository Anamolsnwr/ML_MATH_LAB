About this module

What it is: Descriptive statistics summarize numerical data using the mean, median, variance, and standard deviation.

Why it matters: These measures help you understand your data. Mean and standard deviation are used in z-score standardization, which puts numerical features on comparable scales.

How to use it: Enter at least two numbers separated by commas, then select Calculate. This tool reports sample variance and sample standard deviation.
