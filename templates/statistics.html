Summarize a list of numbers with its average, middle value, and spread.

About this module

What it is: Statistics boils a long list of numbers down to a few meaningful ones: the mean (average), the median (middle value), the variance and standard deviation (how spread out the numbers are).

Why machine learning cares: Before training a model, you need to understand your data. These numbers also drive normalization, which rescales features so that no single feature overpowers the others.

How to use it: Enter your numbers separated by commas (for example 4, 8, 6, 5, 3, 8, 9) and press Run. Variance and standard deviation need at least two numbers.
