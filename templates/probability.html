Work out how likely an event is from the number of ways it can happen.

About this module

What it is: Probability measures how likely something is, on a scale from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain). It is the number of ways you can "win" divided by every possible outcome.

Why machine learning cares: Classifiers rarely just say "yes" or "no". They output a probability, like "82% likely to be spam", and that number is what the model's decisions are built on.

How to use it: Enter how many outcomes count as a win and how many outcomes are possible in total (rolling a 6 on a die is 1 favorable out of 6 total). The program gives the probability, the percentage, and the odds.
